# Definition

Assists are earned whenever a player deals damage onto an opponent that was
eliminated by the damage dealer's team.

Any unhealed damage onto an opponent is eligible for an assist as long as it is
a non-zero amount.

# Approach

The calculation works the same as [damage contribution](/docs/dce).

The difference is that 
except a player's assist stat is incremented by one if they dealt unhealed
damage.

# Definition

Assists are earned whenever a player deals damage that contributed to their team
getting an elimination.

Any unhealed damage onto an opponent is eligible for an assist as long as it is
a non-zero amount.

# Approach

The calculation works exactly the same as [damage contribution](/docs/dce),
except a player's assist stat is incremented by one if they dealt unhealed
damage.
